      About me

      Hola, I'm HP.

      Some people call me El Padre. Online I go by Mezquila.

      I was born in Rauris, in Salzburg's Alps. At a German university I met Adriana, who comes from Jalisco, and fell in love twice: with her, and with the way her country lives.

      In 2014 that love story became a tequila. Padre Azul is still what I spend most of my days on. I'm the co-founder, not the CEO, which means I get to stay close to the bars, the people and the stories.

      Mexico taught me something Europe had forgotten: death is not the enemy of a good life, forgetting death is. The old masters said memento mori. We turned it around. Memento Vivere, remember you must live.

      Padre Azul

      Padre Azul

      Austria meets Mexico.

      Padre Azul is a tequila from Jalisco, born in 2014 from the connection between an Austrian and a Mexican family. Every bottle passes through two hundred hands before it reaches yours.

      The skull on top is called Ramon. He is the face and the spokesperson of the brand, and he is there for a reason: he reminds you that time is short, so share it well.

      The family also includes Padrecito, the younger and more playful one.

      The Padre Manifesto, in short

      People ask me why there is a skull on our bottle. I think the better question is why every other bottle in this industry pretends you will live forever.

      Mexico taught me that death is not the enemy of a good life. Forgetting death is. The old masters said memento mori, remember you must die. We turned it around. Memento Vivere: remember you must live.

      So the skull is a handshake. You face it, you pour, and you pass it to the person across the table. That is the ritual.

      An agave needs eight to ten years to mature, and our Añejo rests eighteen months in oak. Time is the most expensive ingredient we use, and the most expensive thing you will ever spend. Spend it well, with people who matter.

      Remember you must live.

      Second brain

      My second brain

      I run my working life out of one Obsidian vault with Claude sitting on top of it.

      Every morning at 09:07 Vienna time, yesterday's meeting transcripts land in a folder called raw. One command turns them into wiki pages about the people, the markets and the decisions, and files the originals where they belong.

      One page is called hot. It holds what is active right now, and it is the first thing I read each day.

      I'll share the setup, the skills and the mistakes here as I go.

      Skull ritual

      The skull ritual

      Face it, then live.

      1. Open the bottle. The skull on top is called Ramon.
      2. Let him remind you what this moment is worth.
      3. Pour.
      4. When you finish your glass, hand Ramon to the person next to you. It is a promise of eternal friendship. You can't give it back.

      Ramon reminds us that time is a scarce resource, and that we should spend it with friends and family.
