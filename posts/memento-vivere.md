People ask me why there's a skull on our bottle. Wrong question. The right question: why does every bottle in this industry pretend you'll live forever?

Ten years ago, Austria met Mexico. A love story became a tequila. And Mexico taught me something Europe had forgotten: death is not the enemy of a good life. Forgetting death is. The old masters called it memento mori. Remember you must die. We turned it around. Memento Vivere: remember you must live.

That's why the skull sits on top of our bottle. Not decoration. A handshake. When you open Padre Azul, the skull looks back at you and reminds you what this moment is worth. Then you pour. Then you pass the skull to the person across the table.

That's the ritual: face it, then live.

Eight to ten years for the agave to mature. Eighteen months in oak for our Añejo. Time is the most expensive ingredient we use, and the most expensive thing you'll ever spend. So spend it well. With people who matter.

Some say putting a skull on a luxury tequila is crazy. We say: that's Padre Azul.

**Remember you must live.**
